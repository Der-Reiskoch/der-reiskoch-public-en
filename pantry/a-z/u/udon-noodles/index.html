Udon Noodles (饂飩)

Thick Japanese Wheat Noodles

Japanese Noodles

Udon noodles (饂飩 or うどん) are the thickest noodles in Japanese cuisine. They have a soft, almost elastic texture and are made from wheat flour. You can buy udon noodles in pre-cooked or dried form in Asian supermarkets.

Most udon noodles you get in Asian supermarkets here, however, come from China or Korea; original Japanese udon noodles are rather rare. You can find them in shops that specialize in Japanese cuisine or in stores with a larger Japanese food section.

Instant Udon Noodles with Broth

Udon noodles are also sold, like other Asian instant noodles, packaged with instant broth as a quick meal. For example, there is the instant Udon soup by Assi.
