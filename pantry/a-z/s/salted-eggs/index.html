What Are Salted Eggs?

Salted eggs (咸蛋) are preserved eggs that originally come from China and are now common across large parts of East and Southeast Asia. They are usually made from duck eggs that are soaked in a strong brine for several weeks or coated in a paste of salt and clay. Afterwards the egg white tastes very salty, while the yolk turns firm, oily and deep orange.

In China they are called "Xian Dan" (咸蛋), in Vietnam Trứng Muối. In Thailand salted eggs are known as "Khai Khem" (ไข่เค็ม).
