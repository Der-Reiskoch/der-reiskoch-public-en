Samyang Instant Noodles

Korean Ramen in Many Variations

Korean Noodles Instant Product
Table of contents
  • Some Variants
  • The Sauce for the Noodles
  • The Snack for the Noodles

Samyang is a Korean manufacturer of instant ramen noodles. Besides classic instant noodles in ramen form, Samyang is best known for its Buldak Ramen (Buldak Bokkeum Myeon 불닭볶음면), which are prepared with a spicy sauce and come in various flavors.

You can find Samyang products in most Asian supermarkets and by now partly in regular supermarkets as well.

Some Variants

  • Buldak Ramen Noodles¹
  • 2x Spicy Buldak Ramen Noodles¹
  • Buldak Carbonara Ramen Noodles¹
  • Buldak Curry Ramen Noodles¹

The Sauce for the Noodles

If you like the sauce that comes with Buldak Hot Chicken, 2x Spicy Buldak or Buldak Carbonara Ramen, there is good news. Samyang also sells it separately in bottles as Buldak Sauce.

The Snack for the Noodles

For fans of the spicy Buldak Ramen noodles, Samyang has also released a spicy snack. The heat is moderate, though, and for my taste the potato-chip-like rolls are too sweet.
