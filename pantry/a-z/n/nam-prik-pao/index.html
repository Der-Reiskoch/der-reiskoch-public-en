What Is Nam Prik Pao?

“Nam Prik Pao” ( น้ำพริกเผา) is the name of a Thai seasoning paste made from chilies and many other, mostly roasted ingredients. In Asian supermarkets you will often find it in jars labeled “Chili Paste in Oil” or “Chili Paste in Soybean Oil”.

In English-language recipes it is often called “Chili Jam”. The Thai name is made up of the parts Nam Prik ( น้ำพริก) for a paste with chilies and Pao ( เผา) for roasting.

There are countless recipes for Nam Prik Pao, including some that are mixed with ingredients that are unusual for us, such as pork or seafood.

Vegetarian Alternatives

Since traditional Nam Prik Pao also contains roasted shrimp paste, it is not suitable for vegetarian cooking. In the meantime, however, vegetarian or vegan versions are also available ready-made. Mae Pranom, for example, offers a vegetarian version, and Yak Thai offers a “Nam Prik Pao Sam Hed” based on mushrooms.

If you don’t like mushrooms and can’t find anything else in your trusted Asian supermarket, there is also a Chinese chili paste with soybeans, which makes a fairly good substitute. You can also make Nam Prik Pao entirely yourself and leave out the shrimp paste.
