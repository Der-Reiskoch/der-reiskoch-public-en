Green Thai Curry Paste

Paste for Green Curries: Spicy and Aromatic

Thai Curry Paste
Table of contents
  • Usage
  • Comparison Video

You can use this Thai curry paste to prepare green Thai curries, or “Gaeng Khiau Wan” ( ). In Thai it is called “Krueang Gaeng Khiau Wan” ( ) or “Prik Gaeng Khiau Wan” ( ), and very rarely also “Nam Prik Gaeng Khiau Wan” ( ).

Commercially available green curry pastes are generally the spiciest of all Thai curry pastes. The use of kaffir lime zest gives them a slightly bitter-sour note. The paste works best with heartier meats such as beef or pork, which can stand up to its bold flavors.

You can find green curry pastes from various manufacturers in every Asian grocery store and also in many regular supermarkets. I particularly recommend the green curry paste by Yak Thai. It is made with natural ingredients in Bavaria and you can order it online.

With a little effort, you can also make a green curry paste yourself. In my recipe for homemade green curry paste I show you how it’s done.
