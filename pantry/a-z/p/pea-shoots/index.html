Pea Shoots

Young Sprouts for Wok Dishes and Soups

Vegetable Sprouts

Pea shoots are the young shoots of the garden pea or sugar pea (Pisum sativum). In Asian cooking, you usually buy them freshly harvested or soon after cutting. Their flavor is mildly sweet and reminds you of young peas with a slightly grassy note.
