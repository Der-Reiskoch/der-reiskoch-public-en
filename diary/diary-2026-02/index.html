Pad See Ew Goong
Jens2026-02-28

For lunch today, I had a quick Thai Pad See Ew Goong ( ผัดซีอิ๊วกุ้ง ) with rice noodles, shrimp, and broccolini.

Nam Tok Gai
Jens2026-02-27

This evening I prepared grilled chicken as an Isaan salad, which one could call Nam Tok Gai ( น้ำตกไก่ ).

Khanom Jeen Nam Ya
Jens2026-02-23

For lunch today, I once again had Thai Khanom Jeen Nam Ya ( ขนมจีนน้ำยา ) with shredded fish and fish balls. As is usually the case with this dish, the name is not entirely correct in my version, because I used regular rice noodles instead of traditional Khanom Jeen noodles.

Kuai Tiao Plaa
Jens2026-02-22

For lunch today, I prepared a Thai Kuai Tiao Plaa ( ก๋วยเตี๋ยวน้ำใสปลา ) with rice noodles, cod, and fish balls. The recipe once again comes from “Thailand: The Cookbook”

Recommended Thailand: The Cookbook at amazon.de¹

Plaa Tom Kha Min
Jens2026-02-20

For lunch today, I cooked a Plaa Tom Kha Min ( ปลาต้มขมิ้น ) from southern Thailand. The recipe for this simple fish soup with turmeric comes from the book “Sabai” by Pailin Chongchitnant.

Recommended Sabai at amazon.de¹

Laap Hed
Jens2026-02-17

This evening I had a spicy and delicious Thai Laap Hed ( ลาบเห็ด ) with minced meat, shimeji mushrooms, and fresh herbs.

Pad Phed Nuea
Jens2026-02-16

For lunch today, I prepared a quick Pad Phed Nuea ( ผัดเผ็ดเนื้อ ) with beef, baby corn, yard long beans, and shimeji mushrooms.

Aep Moo and Mok Gai
Jens2026-02-15

For lunch today, I prepared two Thai dishes that are both prepared in banana leaves but are still quite different. Aep Moo ( แอ็บหมู ) from northern Thailand, where pork is mixed with a paste and herbs and grilled in banana leaves, and Mok Gai ( หมกไก่ ) from Isaan, where marinated chicken is steamed in banana leaves.

Yum Thua Nog
Jens2026-02-11

This evening I made another Thai Yum Thua Nog ( ยำถั่วงอก ) with mung bean sprouts, this time in a variation with fish sauce, lime, and palm sugar. I mixed the sprouts with carrot strips and peanuts.

Gaeng Sap Nok
Jens2026-02-09

For lunch today, I had a simple Thai Gaeng Sap Nok ( แกงสับนก ) with minced turkey, yard long beans, and plenty of fresh ginger. The recipe comes from the book “Thai Food” by David Thompson.

Recommended Thai Food at amazon.de¹

Sai Krok and Salads
Jens2026-02-06

This evening I tried the very tasty Sai Krok ( ไส้กรอก ) from Yak Thai. To go with it there was a Laap Gai ( ลาบไก่ ) and, for a bit of acidity, a Yum Som O ( ยำส้มโอ ) with pomelo.

Yum Thua Nog
Jens2026-02-04

I actually thought I knew all Thai yum salads by now, but then I came across this southern Thai Yum Thua Nog ( ยำถั่วงอก ) with mung bean sprouts. This simple salad, in which the sprouts are mixed with shallots and fresh herbs in a dressing of coconut milk and vinegar, turned out to be extremely delicious.

Khai Pad Thaeng Gwa Sai Moo
Jens2026-02-04

I decided to cook more from the book “Thai Food” by David Thompson. So for lunch today I had this very simple Khai Pad Thaeng Gwa Sai Moo ( ไข่ผัดแตงกวาใส่หมู ) with pork belly, cucumber, and eggs.

Thai Food¹

Mee Kati Isaan
Jens2026-02-03

For lunch today I made Mee Kati Isaan ( หมี่กะทิอีสาน ) with rice noodles, pork belly, and minced pork. The recipe for this hearty Mee Kati variation comes from the book “Hawker Fare"s by James Syhabout.

Hawker Fare¹

Pad Phed Gai Thua Fak Yao
Jens2026-02-02

During the lunch break today, there was a quick Thai Pad Phed Gai Thua Fak Yao ( ผัดเผ็ดไก่ถั่วฝักยาว ) with chicken and yard long beans.

Khaep Moo and Yum Thaeng Gwa
Jens2026-02-01

After all the delicious food last week, this evening we simply had a Thai cucumber salad or Yum Thaeng Gwa ( ยำแตงกวา ) with some pork cracklings on the side.
