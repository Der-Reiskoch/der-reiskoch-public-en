Two Thai Dishes with Egg
Jens2026-01-28

This evening there were two simple Thai dishes with egg. First Bon Khai Tom ( ) with crushed hard-boiled eggs and then Khai Pam ( ). This dish consists of beaten eggs that are grilled together with spices in a banana leaf.

Thua Fak Yao Pad Sechuan
Jens2026-01-23

This evening I tried another quick recipe from the book “Classic Thai Stir Fry Dishes” by Hanuman Aspler. The Thua Fak Yao Pad Sechuan ( ) with yard long beans and Sichuan pepper really reminded me of the “Gān Biān Sì Jì Dòu” (干煸四季豆) from two weeks ago, and indeed the dish probably originated from it.

Recommended Classic Thai Stir Fry Dishes at amazon.de¹

Gai Tom Kha Min
Jens2026-01-08

In the latest newsletter from “Hot Thai Kitchen” Pailin presented five different Thai soups. I tried the Gai Tom Kha Min ( ) with chicken in a broth made from turmeric root, lemongrass, galangal, and kaffir lime leaves for lunch today.
