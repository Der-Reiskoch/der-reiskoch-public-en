Pad Fak Kra Thin Mhu Sap
Jens2026-08-05

For dinner today I used the pods of the white popinac to prepare a . The idea sounded better than the result, because the pods were surprisingly tough and fibrous.

The subsequent research explained why. In Thailand, wok dishes apparently use mostly the young shoot tips or the seeds rather than the mature pods. At least now I know from my own experience why no recipes using the pods can be found. The remaining pods go again as a side dish with a Nam Prik or a salad.
