Green Curry with Sides
Jens2026-04-10

This evening I had a Thai Gaeng Keow Wan Nuea ( แกงเขียวหวานเนื้อ ) with beef, baby eggplants and green asparagus. As a side I also prepared Ajaad Thaeng Gwa ( อาจาดแตงกวา ). For seasoning there was Prik Nam Plaa ( พริกน้ำปลา ) as well as pickled Thai eggplants.

Ba Mie Mhu Daeng
Jens2026-04-08

During my lunch break today I used up the leftover Moo Daeng in a Ba Mee Moo Daeng ( บะหมี่หมูแดง ) soup.

Three Dishes from Northern Thailand
Jens2026-04-05

This evening I made Sup Pak Gai ( ซุปผัก ) from Italian puntarelle, Gai Thod Thakrai ( ไก่ทอดตะไคร้ ) and pork with spicy Nam Prik Noom ( น้ำพริกหนุ่ม ). As a side I had Riceberry for the first time instead of jasmine rice or sticky rice. That is what happens when you listen to the Sabai Talk Podcast about northern Thai cuisine while out hiking.

Pad Prik Khing
Jens2026-04-02

During my lunch break, I made a Thai Pad Prik Khing ( ผัดพริกขิง ) with thinly sliced pork belly today.

Khao Tom Mhu Sap
Jens2026-04-01

When there is no bread left in the house, Thai breakfast fills the gap in the form of a rice soup with minced pork balls, Khao Tom Moo Sap ( ข้าวต้มหมูสับ ).
