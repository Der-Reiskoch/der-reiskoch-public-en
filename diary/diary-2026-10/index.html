Kimchi Fried Rice
Jens2026-10-05

For lunch today I used the rest of the Mannheim kimchi to make Korean kimchi fried rice. Because of the strong sauerkraut note of this kimchi from the Palatinate region, the dish did not really convince us. I will have to try it again with real Korean kimchi. As a banchan I served Gungchae Jangajji ( ), which is Gong Choi pickled in soy sauce.

Or Lam Pik Gai
Jens2026-10-02

So that I do not catch a cold as well, I also had a chicken soup for myself this evening in the form of an Or Lam Pik Gai ( ) from Isaan. The paste already made it quite spicy, but I also seasoned it with the “Crispy Chili Pepper Punch” chili paste from Yak Thai. The pepper flavor went very well with this Isaan dish.
