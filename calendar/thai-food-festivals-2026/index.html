Thai Thai Festivals

In 2026, there will once again be plenty of Thai food festivals in Germany, Austria and Switzerland. Perfect for discovering truly authentic Thai cuisine and immerse yourself in the culture. To make sure you don’t lose track, I’m collecting all the dates for you in my calendar, so you’re guaranteed not to miss a festival in your area.

I am not the organiser of these events and therefore cannot guarantee that the Thai Food Festivals listed here will actually take place. Please check directly with the organisers before attending one of the events.
